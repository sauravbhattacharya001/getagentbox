🔧 Common Solutions

Click a topic to expand step-by-step instructions.

🤖 Bot Not Responding

AgentBox isn't replying to your messages

  1. Check system status for outages
  2. Send /start to reinitialize the bot
  3. Clear your Telegram cache (Settings → Data & Storage)
  4. Try sending a simple message like "Hello"
  5. If still not working, submit a ticket below

🧠 Memory Issues

Bot forgot something or remembers wrong info

  1. Send /memory to view what AgentBox remembers
  2. Use /forget [topic] to remove specific memories
  3. Use /reset to clear all conversation history
  4. Note: memory improves with more conversations

⏰ Reminder Not Firing

Scheduled reminder didn't arrive

  1. Check your timezone setting with /settings
  2. Ensure Telegram notifications are enabled for the bot
  3. Try setting a test reminder: "Remind me in 1 minute to test"
  4. Use /reminders to list all pending reminders

📷 Image Analysis Failed

Bot can't analyze your image or photo

  1. Ensure the image is under 20MB
  2. Send as a photo, not a file/document
  3. Try re-sending with a clear question about the image
  4. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP

💳 Billing Questions

Free tier limits, upgrades, or payment issues

  1. Free tier: 20 messages/day, resets at midnight UTC
  2. Check usage with /usage
  3. Upgrade anytime with /upgrade
  4. For payment issues, submit a ticket with your Telegram username

🔒 Privacy & Data

Questions about your data and privacy

  1. Your data is isolated — no one else can access it
  2. Use /export to download all your data
  3. Use /delete-account to permanently delete everything
  4. See our Privacy Center for full details
