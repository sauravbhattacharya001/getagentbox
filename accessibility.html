1. Our Commitment

AgentBox is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We believe that everyone — regardless of disability, impairment, or assistive technology — should be able to use our products and services with ease.

We continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards across our website and Telegram-based agent interface.

2. Standards We Follow

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. These guidelines cover a wide range of recommendations for making web content more accessible, including:

  • Perceivable — Information and UI components must be presentable in ways users can perceive (text alternatives, captions, adaptable layouts, sufficient contrast).
  • Operable — UI components and navigation must be operable (keyboard accessible, enough time, no seizure triggers, clear navigation).
  • Understandable — Information and UI operation must be understandable (readable, predictable, input assistance).
  • Robust — Content must be robust enough to work with current and future assistive technologies.

3. Accessibility Features

Our website and product include the following accessibility features:

Navigation & Structure

  • Semantic HTML throughout all pages (<nav>, <main>, <section>, headings hierarchy)
  • Skip-to-content links where applicable
  • ARIA landmarks and labels for screen readers
  • Keyboard-navigable menus and interactive elements
  • Visible focus indicators on all focusable elements

Visual Design

  • Light and dark mode toggle to suit different visual needs
  • Color contrast ratios meeting WCAG AA minimums (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text)
  • Responsive design that adapts to zoom levels up to 200%
  • No information conveyed solely through color
  • Resizable text without loss of functionality

Content

  • Descriptive alt text for meaningful images
  • Clear link text that makes sense out of context
  • Plain language wherever possible
  • Consistent navigation across pages

Telegram Agent

  • Text-based interface compatible with all screen readers
  • No time limits on interactions
  • Clear, structured responses from the AI agent
  • Works with Telegram's built-in accessibility features

5. Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some areas are still being improved:

  • Third-party embeds: Some embedded content (e.g., video players, analytics widgets) may not fully meet accessibility standards. We work with vendors to improve this.
  • PDF documents: Older PDF resources may not be fully tagged for screen readers. We are converting these to accessible HTML where possible.
  • Complex data visualizations: Charts on the ROI Calculator and Status pages include text alternatives, but may not convey all nuances to screen reader users.

We actively track and resolve accessibility issues. If you encounter a barrier, please let us know.

6. How We Test

Our accessibility testing process includes:

  • Automated scanning using axe-core, Lighthouse, and WAVE
  • Manual testing with keyboard navigation and screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver)
  • Browser testing across Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge
  • Periodic audits when major features or pages are added
  • User feedback incorporated into continuous improvement

7. Feedback & Contact

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of AgentBox. If you encounter any barriers or have suggestions, please contact us:

  • Email: accessibility@getagentbox.com
  • General support: Contact page

We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within 5 business days and to resolve reported issues within 30 days where feasible.

8. Enforcement & Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response to your accessibility concern, you may escalate through:

  • Your national disability rights organization
  • The relevant government enforcement body in your jurisdiction
  • In the US: File an ADA complaint
  • In the EU: Contact your country's national enforcement body under the Web Accessibility Directive
