Frequently Asked Questions

How long does cookie tracking last?
Our referral cookies last 90 days. If someone clicks your link and signs up within 90 days, you get the commission — even if they close the tab and come back later.
When do commissions start?
Commissions start from the first paid invoice. Free trial periods don't count, but once a customer converts to paid, you earn from day one.
Can I refer myself?
No. Self-referrals are not eligible for commissions. Our system detects duplicate accounts automatically.
What counts as an "active" referral for tier upgrades?
A referral is "active" if they have a paid subscription that has been active for at least 30 days. Churned or cancelled customers don't count toward your tier qualification.
Is there a cap on earnings?
No cap. You earn recurring commissions for every active customer you refer, with no upper limit. Some of our top partners earn over $5,000/month.
Can agencies white-label AgentBox for their clients?
Yes — Gold partners can access the white-label program. This lets you offer AgentBox under your own brand with custom domain, colors, and logo. Contact your partner manager for setup.
