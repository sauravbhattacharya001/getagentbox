Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join the affiliate program? ▼
Anyone! Content creators, developers, bloggers, consultants, newsletters — if you have an audience that could benefit from an AI agent, you're a great fit.
How long do cookies last? ▼
Our affiliate cookies last 90 days. If someone clicks your link and signs up within 90 days, you get credit for the referral.
Are commissions recurring? ▼
Yes! You earn commissions for as long as the referred customer stays subscribed. It's truly passive income.
When do I get paid? ▼
Payouts happen monthly (Starter), bi-weekly (Growth), or weekly (Elite) once you hit the $50 minimum threshold. We support PayPal, Stripe, and direct bank transfers.
Can I promote AgentBox on social media? ▼
Absolutely! We encourage it. We provide social media kits and pre-made content to make it easy. Just follow our brand guidelines and be transparent about the affiliate relationship.
Is there a limit to how much I can earn? ▼
No cap on earnings. The more subscribers you refer, the higher your tier and commission rate. Our top affiliates earn $5,000+/month.
