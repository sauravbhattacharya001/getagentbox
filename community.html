🤝 Community Guidelines

  • 💚

    Be Kind & Respectful

    Treat everyone with respect. We're all here to learn and build together. No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.

  • 🔍

    Search Before Asking

    Check the FAQ, docs, and previous discussions before posting a question. It helps keep the community efficient.

  • 🎯

    Stay On Topic

    Keep discussions relevant to AgentBox and AI agents. Use the right channels for different topics.

  • 🔒

    Protect Privacy

    Never share API keys, personal data, or private information. Report any privacy concerns to moderators.

  • 🌱

    Help Others Grow

    Share your knowledge freely. Detailed answers help not just the asker but everyone who finds it later.
