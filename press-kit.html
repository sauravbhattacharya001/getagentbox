  • Use "AgentBox" with capital A and B
  • Use our brand colors faithfully

  • Describe us as "AI assistant for Telegram"

  • Write "Agentbox", "agentbox", or "AGENTBOX"
  • Alter the logo colors or proportions
  • Use our brand to imply endorsement
