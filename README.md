## ✨ Features

- **🧠 It remembers you** — Your agent learns your preferences, context, and history. No more repeating yourself.
- **🔍 Web search built in** — Ask anything about the real world — it searches the web and gives you a straight answer.
- **⏰ Reminders & scheduling** — "Remind me in 30 minutes to check the oven." Done. It just works.
- **📷 Understands images** — Send a photo and ask about it. Screenshots, documents, memes — it sees what you see.
- **🎤 Voice messages** — Too lazy to type? Send a voice note. It'll understand and respond.
- **🔒 Your data, your agent** — Private workspace for each user. Your conversations stay yours.
