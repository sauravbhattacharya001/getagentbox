Privacy FAQ

🤖
Is my data used to train AI models?
▼

No. Your conversations are never used for model training. Messages are sent to the AI provider for real-time processing only — they are not stored or used to improve models.

👀
Can your team read my messages?
▼

Messages are encrypted at rest. Our team does not access individual conversations. The only exception would be if required by law with a valid legal order — and we'd notify you if legally permitted.

🌍
Where is my data stored?
▼

Data is stored on encrypted servers in the United States (AWS). All data transfers use TLS 1.3 encryption. We do not replicate data to other regions.

⚖️
Are you GDPR/CCPA compliant?
▼

Yes. We follow GDPR and CCPA principles for all users regardless of location: data minimization, purpose limitation, right to access, right to erasure, and data portability. You don't need to be in the EU or California to exercise these rights.

🚨
What happens if there's a data breach?
▼

We will notify affected users within 72 hours via Telegram message, disclose the scope and nature of the breach, and publish a transparency report. Because data is encrypted at rest, a server breach alone would not expose message contents.

👶
Is AgentBox safe for children?
▼

AgentBox is designed for users 13 and older (in compliance with Telegram's age requirement). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn a child's data was collected, we will delete it promptly.
